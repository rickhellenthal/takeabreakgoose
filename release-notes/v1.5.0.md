## 📞 The goose stays away during calls

No more goose walking across your screen in the middle of a meeting. During a focus session, **Do not disturb turns on by itself when you're in a call**, and turns off again when the call is over.

- **What counts as a call:** your camera is in use, or a meeting app like **Teams, Zoom, Webex, Slack or Google Meet** (in your browser) uses your microphone.
- **What doesn't:** voice chat in games, Discord or Steam. The goose still reminds you to take a break while you're gaming.
- A break that comes up during the call waits until it's over. If your session ends during a call, the goose says goodbye afterwards.
- While it's on, the tray icon and the app say **"In a call"**. The first time it happens, a notification lets you know.
- Want to show the goose to someone during a call? Turn Do not disturb off, and it stays off until the call is over.
- Don't want this? Turn off **"Turn on Do not disturb during calls"** under **Settings → General**.

## ✏️ A tidier Settings tab

The messages no longer fill up the Settings tab. The **Messages** card now just shows how many there are, and **Edit messages** opens them on a page of their own, with its own **Save**. Use **← Settings** at the top to go back.

## ☕ Support the goose

The goose is free, and it stays that way. If it helps you, there's now a **Buy me a coffee** link at the bottom of the Settings tab.

## Install

- **`TakeABreakGoose-Setup-1.5.0.exe`**: run it to install or update. No admin rights are needed. If the goose is running, the installer asks to close it first.
- **`TakeABreakGoose-1.5.0-portable.zip`**: just the app. Unzip it anywhere and run it.

Works on Windows 10 and 11. If you already have 1.4.0, the Settings tab will tell you about this update.

The app isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
