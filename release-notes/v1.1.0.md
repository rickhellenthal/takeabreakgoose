## 💤 The goose naps during your break

When you click **Okay, break time!**, the goose no longer walks off. It stays with you for the whole break:

- It **sits down on the taskbar and takes a nap**: legs tucked in, head drooped, eyes closed, and little *z z Z*s floating up.
- A small card above it shows a **ring that fills up** as your break goes on, with a countdown of the time left. Hover over the goose to see when the nap ends.
- When the break is over, the goose **wakes up**, says *"Break's over, welcome back!"* and walks off.

If you set the break length to 0 in Settings, the goose walks off right away like before.

## Fixes

- The break and the countdown to your next reminder now start the moment you click **Okay, break time!**, not when the goose has walked off the screen.
- If your focus session ends while the goose is napping, the *"Session complete!"* goose now comes right after the nap. Before, that message was lost.

## Install

- **`TakeABreakGoose-Setup-1.1.0.exe`**: run it to install or update. No admin rights are needed. If the goose is running, the installer asks to close it first.
- **`TakeABreakGoose-1.1.0-portable.zip`**: just the app. Unzip it anywhere and run it.

The app isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
