## 🔕 Do not disturb

In a meeting? Turn on **Do not disturb** on your session card, or from the tray icon.

- The goose stays away, but your timer keeps running.
- A break that comes up in the meantime waits. As soon as you turn Do not disturb off, the goose comes by.
- Turning it on while the goose is on screen sends it away right away.

## 🎉 The goose signs off

When your session reaches its end time, the goose drops by in its party hat to tell you how long you focused and that it's time to wrap up. If Do not disturb is on, it waits politely until you turn it off.

## ⏳ Choose your break length per session

The start card now has **Each break lasts**: *5, 10 or 15 minutes, or Custom*. It starts at your default, which you can change in **Settings → Default break length**.

## 🪿 A more patient goose

- **No more missed breaks:** the goose waits until you answer instead of leaving after a while. If you didn't notice it, it's still there when you look up.
- **Time away counts:** if you were away from your computer for at least a break's length while the goose waited, that counts as your break. When you're back, it asks if you're ready to focus.
- **Only during a session:** break reminders now only happen during a focus session. *Take a break now*, *Pause* and *Stop session* only show up in the tray menu while a session runs. The *Summon goose now* button in Settings is gone.

## ✨ Smaller things

- **Version number:** you can see which version you have at the bottom of the Settings tab. When a newer one is out, you'll see a note with a download link there too.
- **Scrolling:** each tab now starts at the top instead of where the last one was scrolled to.

## Install

- **`TakeABreakGoose-Setup-1.3.0.exe`**: run it to install or update. No admin rights are needed. If the goose is running, the installer asks to close it first.
- **`TakeABreakGoose-1.3.0-portable.zip`**: just the app. Unzip it anywhere and run it.

The app isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
