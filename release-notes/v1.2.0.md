## 👋 The goose waits until you're back

When your break is over, the goose no longer just walks off. It keeps napping until you use your computer again, then wakes up and asks: *"Welcome back! Ready to focus again?"*

- Click **Ready to focus** and the countdown to your next break starts from that moment, not while you were still away.
- Not quite ready? Ask for **a few more minutes** and the goose naps a little longer.
- Until you answer, no new break reminders come in.

## 💤 Ending a break early

Need to get back to work before your break is over? **Click the napping goose.** It's not thrilled about being woken up (and will tell you so), but it lets you choose between **Keep resting** and **End my break**.

## ⏱️ A quicker way to start a session

- Pick your break interval with one click: **15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes**, or **Custom** for your own number.
- **Set an end time** is now tucked away under a link, so a quick session is just two clicks. When you open it, you can end the session:
  - **after a while**, like 2 hours
  - **at a time of day**, like 17:00, using your computer's clock
- A preview shows when the session will end, like *"The session ends at 17:00, in 2 h 14 min."*

## 🪿 "Always on" is now "start a focus session automatically"

There used to be two ways to keep the goose around: *Always on* in Settings, and a session without an end time. They're now one thing.

- In **Settings → General**, turn on **Start a focus session when the app starts**. Together with *Start with Windows*, the goose keeps an eye on you from the moment you sign in.
- It's a normal session, so you can see the countdown, pause it and stop it.
- **Stop session** now really means quiet.
- If you had *Always on* turned on, this is switched on for you, with the same interval.

## 🎩 Wardrobe

**Send this goose** now sends a goose that just shows off its outfit (*"Ta-da!"*). It doesn't affect your session's timer anymore.

## Install

- **`TakeABreakGoose-Setup-1.2.0.exe`**: run it to install or update. No admin rights are needed. If the goose is running, the installer asks to close it first.
- **`TakeABreakGoose-1.2.0-portable.zip`**: just the app. Unzip it anywhere and run it.

The app isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
