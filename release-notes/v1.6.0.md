## 😴 The goose knows when you're skipping your break

The goose naps on your taskbar during your break. Now it keeps one eye half open.

- **Still at the computer?** If you keep using the mouse or keyboard during your break, the goose half wakes up: it lifts its head, opens one sleepy eye and looks a bit confused. Leave the computer alone and it dozes off again.
- **Keep at it** and it asks whether you're actually taking your break. **I'm going!** sends it back to sleep.
- **Ending your break early?** Whether the goose caught you or you clicked it, it asks one more time, and it doesn't hide what it thinks of that. Go ahead anyway and it walks off in a huff.
- Got up after all, without answering? The goose quietly goes back to sleep.

## ⏱️ Setting an end time is quicker

- When you open **Set an end time**, **End at** is already filled in with the next quarter hour.
- **−15 min** and **+15 min** buttons move the end time. In the time box, the **↑** and **↓** keys move it by 5 minutes, or by 1 minute with **Shift**.
- **End after** uses the same buttons as the rest of the start card: **1 h** to **4 h**, or **Other…** to type your own.
- The option you didn't pick is greyed out, and closing **Set an end time** forgets what you picked there.
- Picking **Other…** selects the number in the box, so you can type over it straight away.

## 🔕 Snoozing is optional

Don't want to be tempted? Under **Settings → Breaks**, untick **Let me snooze for … min** and the goose no longer offers a Snooze button when it's time for a break.

## 🛠️ Fixes and small changes

- **Starting with Windows** stays in sync with Task Manager. If you disabled the goose under **Startup apps** in Task Manager, the setting now shows it as off, and turning it back on works again. The setting is now called **Start Take a Break Goose when my computer starts**.
- The message *"Step away from the keyboard. The bug will still be there in five minutes."* now reads *"Step away from the screen. Whatever you're working on will still be there in five minutes."*

## Install

- **`TakeABreakGoose-Setup-1.6.0.exe`**: run it to install or update. No admin rights are needed. If the goose is running, the installer asks to close it first.
- **`TakeABreakGoose-1.6.0-portable.zip`**: just the app. Unzip it anywhere and run it.

Works on Windows 10 and 11. If you already have the goose, the Settings tab will tell you about this update.

The app isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
