## 🇳🇱 The goose speaks Dutch

Take a Break Goose now speaks English and Dutch. Pick one under **Language** in the Settings tab: the app switches right away, and the goose's own messages switch along. Messages you wrote or changed yourself stay as they are.

## ✨ Smaller things

- **Reading counts as working.** Reading or watching a video without touching the mouse no longer counts as being away after a minute. Being away now counts as your break after at least 5 minutes, or your break's length if that's longer.
- **One goose at a time from the Wardrobe.** While a goose is on screen, the Wardrobe's buttons wait until it has left, and say why.
- **Buttons show when you point at them**, with the same warm tint as the choices.
- **The Sunday bonnet** no longer covers the goose's eye.

## Install

On Windows:

- **`TakeABreakGoose-Setup-1.9.0.exe`**: run it to install or update. No admin rights are needed. If the goose is running, the installer asks to close it first.
- **`TakeABreakGoose-1.9.0-portable.zip`**: just the app. Unzip it anywhere and run it.

Works on Windows 10 and 11. If you already have the goose, the Settings tab will tell you about this update. The app isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

On a Mac (macOS 12 or later):

- **`TakeABreakGoose-1.9.0-mac-apple-silicon.dmg`** for a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later), **`TakeABreakGoose-1.9.0-mac-intel.dmg`** for an Intel Mac.
- Open it and drag **Take a Break Goose** onto **Applications**, then open it from there.
- **"Take a Break Goose can't be opened"?** The app isn't signed by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open **Terminal** and run this once:

  ```bash
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Take a Break Goose.app" && open "/Applications/Take a Break Goose.app"
  ```
